In the DC Comics universe, Thomas Wayne was a wealthy philanthropist and lawyer who was murdered in front of his young son Bruce (who would later become Batman) on a dark and rainy night in Crime Alley. The murder occurred when Bruce was just eight years old.

Martha Wayne, Bruce's mother, was also killed during the same event, leaving their son an orphan. The gruesome nature of their deaths had a profound impact on young Bruce, who was traumatized by the experience.

Thomas Wayne's death served as a catalyst for Bruce's desire to become a crimefighter, driven by his determination to protect others from suffering the same fate as his parents. This trauma also fueled his intense anger and need for justice, ultimately leading him to don the cape and cowl of Batman.

The murder of Thomas and Martha Wayne is often referred to as the "origin story" of Batman's creation, setting the stage for the character's epic struggles against evil in Gotham City.
